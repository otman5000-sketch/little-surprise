I know you might be wondering where all of this is coming from, so let me tell you the story of how this idea was born.

I did visit the beach the next day, as my psy recommended. I walked along the shore, even did some running, but my mind kept overthinking, as it tends to. At some point, I decided to just sit down.

And then an idea did hit me. A crazy one, of course. You know me already. Honestly, I wanted to swim, to dive as deep as I could. But I didn’t. It wasn’t because the water was cold. It was more about not having a place to leave my clothes. Trust me, I would have.

But in that moment, I thought of you. I smiled. I remembered something you said about going to the beach with someone you care about and trust. Not to talk, just to be there together.

I couldn’t help but imagine you beside me. I imagined your hands, and I wanted to hold them. I remembered every time you showed me your cute nails, and I couldn’t help but want to hold them. Maybe because it was cold and I wanted to warm you up.

I never really thought of the beach as something special before, but because of you, it was.

And then I remembered a question you asked me once. “Why are you doing all of this? We’re not in a relationship.” For the first time, I just sat there. I didn’t overthink it. The question itself was enough. Just being there, floating with the moment.

I wasn’t looking for an answer.

I started remembering moments. Random ones. You. Us. Your stubbornness. Your cuteness.
I remembered moments when I thought of you even after we stopped talking, and I thought about why I always wanted to be so helpful to you, to be there for you.

I thought of my favorite reel you once sent me, one I don’t think you even remember. But for me, it meant the world.
“HUG ME UNTIL I SMELL LIKE YOU.”
That line has made me smile more times than I can count.

I remembered the day I made you sick because we talked until such a late hour.
I remembered the day I was incredibly grateful to your grandma for being there so we could talk on a call. She couldn’t have made me any happier.
I remembered us talking about our childhood memories.

I also remembered the day you were talking about building a website for your professor. I remembered telling you, if you want, I can help. If you’re busy with other presentations, I’m down.
By the way, at the time, I didn’t even know if it was actually feasible to create a website. I had never done that before. But I wanted to learn it just for you.

Then I smiled and said to myself:
You know, Outman, you wouldn’t have been this excited to do it for your own presentation, you lazy. You would’ve found the easiest way out for the professor.
But for her, you would’ve made damn sure it was the best you could make, learning something completely new, something you don’t even care about, just for her.

And that’s when my one and only question was answered.
Why?
Because I want to.

I expected nothing back.
It was a genuine feeling, one I had never felt before. One that made me just sit there, listen to music, and chill. I felt comfortable after answering that question. Relaxed. Free.

I remembered something you once said: this is life, it has highs and lows.
Somehow, I realized I care more about your lows. I want to be there for you in your lows, and I want you to be with me in my highs.
Why?
Because that’s what I want.

So I decided to build this website.

First, to know for a fact whether I was just bluffing when I said I’d build a website for you.
Hahahah. Turns out, I wasn’t. I’m honestly impressed that I managed to do it in a single day.

Second, to do something special, simply because I felt like it.

I wasn’t even expecting to write a text like this. I originally planned to make quizzes and challenges, something light and not very expressive, themed around the fox and the bunny.
But oh well, it ended up like this, because this felt more true. This is the way I can honestly express my feelings, without hiding behind a game. It felt real.

Usually, Valentine’s gifts end with a question: would you be my Valentine?
I was planning to say, would you be my mogayo to this bogayo?
But I don’t want to do that anymore.

I’m sure you have questions, but I’ll let you answer them the same way I did: by yourself.

I’m doing all of this while expecting nothing back.
I’ve never been more confident in saying that I did this just to make you smile, the same way you’ve made me smile, so many times.
